Select products or templates then click on Merge products in Action
menu. Select Destination product in which need to merge all other
products Then click on Merge Product, it will merge all the products.

When **Keep dominant product putaway rules** is enabled (the default), the
product-specific putaway rules of duplicate SKUs are removed before merging.
The destination product keeps its rules and becomes the dominant SKU for
putaway. Category-level rules are not changed.
